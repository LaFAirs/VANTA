# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in VANTA a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful, constructive, and professional.
- No harassment, trolling, insulting comments, or unwelcome advances.
- Respect differing viewpoints; focus on technical merit.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal,
at maintainer discretion. Report issues via GitHub Security Advisory or the
contact in `DeveloperConfig.swift`.
